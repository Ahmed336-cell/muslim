## Full description (≤ 4000)

### en
```
Sabeelak is a calm, complete companion for a Muslim's day — prayer, Quran, remembrance and learning, in Arabic and English. No ads, no account, no tracking.

PRAYER
• Accurate prayer times for your city or your location, with a live countdown
• Adhan notifications with several well-known voices
• Mark your prayers and see your progress over the weeks
• Qibla compass
• Traveller mode: shortened and combined prayers when you are away from home

QURAN
• Read the Mushaf page by page, as in print
• Listen to renowned reciters, with repeat and a sleep timer
• Tafsir for every ayah
• Hifz: repeat, hide and test yourself, and track what you have memorised
• Daily wird and khatmah plan — finish the Quran in 7 days to a year

ADHKAR & DUAS
• Morning adhkar from Fajr to Dhuhr, evening adhkar from Dhuhr to midnight, then the adhkar before sleep — shown at the right time
• Tap-to-count cards for every dhikr
• The full Hisn al-Muslim (Fortress of the Muslim)
• Tasbih counter
• The 99 Names of Allah with meanings
• Ruqyah from the Quran and Sunnah

HADITH
• Nawawi's Forty, Forty Hadith Qudsi and Riyad as-Salihin, in Arabic and English
• Search, browse by chapter, save, copy and share

YOUR YEAR
• Hijri calendar with the Islamic occasions
• Ramadan: suhoor and iftar times, fasting tracker
• Recommended fasts on the calendar: the white days, Ashura and Arafah
• Friday: Surat al-Kahf and the hour of response
• Qada fasts, fidyah and kaffarah tracker

GIVING
• Zakat calculator
• Sadaqah journal with a monthly goal and a Friday reminder

LEARN
• Step-by-step wudu and salah lessons with short quizzes
• Tajweed basics: noon and meem rules, madd, qalqalah
• Kids' corner: Arabic letters, short surahs and daily duas

AT NIGHT
• Bedtime recitations: al-Mulk, as-Sajdah, Ayat al-Kursi, the end of al-Baqarah and the three Quls
• Sleep timer that gently fades the recitation out

HOME-SCREEN WIDGETS
Next prayer, a verse, a dua, a Name of Allah, a hadith, today's Hijri date and a tasbih counter.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No sign-in, no ads, no analytics. Your prayer log, counts, bookmarks and settings stay on your phone. Most features work offline.

Sabeelak (سبيلك) means "your path". May it help you on yours.
```
